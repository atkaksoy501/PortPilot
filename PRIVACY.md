# PortPilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

PortPilot does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit personal information.

PortPilot runs locally on your Windows device as a PowerToys Command Palette extension. It scans local TCP listening ports in the 3000-10000 range and displays local process details such as port number, process name, process ID, and protocol. This information is used only inside the app to help you identify and, after confirmation, terminate a selected local process.

PortPilot does not send this information to Atakan Aksoy, Microsoft, or any third party. PortPilot does not include analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking, cloud sync, account login, or remote data storage.

PortPilot may request the `runFullTrust` capability because Command Palette desktop extensions need full-trust execution to inspect local processes and terminate a user-selected process after confirmation.

If you contact the developer for support, any information you choose to provide in that communication is used only to respond to your request.
